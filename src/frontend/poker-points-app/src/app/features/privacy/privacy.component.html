Privacy policy

Last updated September 19, 2026

Estimagator helps teams estimate work together. This page explains how this service uses information when you create an account, join a session, or connect Jira.

Information we store

Registered accounts include an email address, display name, password hash, and authentication records. Guest participation includes a display name and session identity. Session records include participants, story titles and links, votes, estimates, and session history.

When you connect Jira, we store encrypted authorization tokens, accessible site details, field selections, and imported issue identifiers, summaries, links, and estimates. We also record publishing attempts, including the acting user, value, time, and result. We do not import issue descriptions or Jira user profiles. Issue summaries may contain personal information supplied by your team.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to sign you in, send account verification and password-reset emails, run shared estimation sessions, retain history, and carry out requested Jira imports and estimate updates. Jira receives requests under the connected user's permissions; publishing an estimate requires an explicit action.

People with a session's access code can join it and see shared session content, including imported Jira summaries and revealed votes. Authorized participants can view session history. Import only content suitable for that audience. Service administrators can access stored account and session records to operate and support the service.

Hosting and email infrastructure process information needed to deliver the service. Connecting Jira also involves Atlassian. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which causes your browser to send network information, such as your IP address, to Google.

Browser storage and logs

Browser storage remembers your sign-in, session identity, and theme preference. The Jira connection flow uses a temporary security cookie. Jira tokens are stored on the server, not in browser storage. Server logs can include IP addresses, request details, errors, and story titles or links for operation and troubleshooting.

Retention and your choices

Account and session records do not currently have a scheduled automatic deletion period; they remain until removed through administration. Closing a session does not delete its history. Backup copies may remain after records are removed from the running service.

You can disconnect Jira in your profile to remove its stored credentials. Imported stories, estimates, and publishing history remain. You can separately revoke the grant in your Atlassian account's Connected apps settings. You can participate as a guest without connecting Jira, and clear this site's browser storage through your browser settings.

Privacy questions and requests

Contact Ras Fincher at ras@rhymeswithjazz.com to ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information. Include enough detail to identify your account or session, but do not send passwords, authorization tokens, or confidential Jira content.
